Vendor partnerships

Three ways to engage.

Steelline works as a vendor partner to organizations, staffing firms, and prime contractors. Each engagement is scoped in writing before work begins.

01

C2C engagements

Corp-to-corp contracts through staffing partners or directly with your organization. Defined scope and timeline, with no headcount, benefits, or long-term commitment added.

02

Fractional engagements

Ongoing Azure administration and security capacity on a set monthly allocation, for teams that need consistent senior coverage without a full-time hire.

03

White-label services

Delivery under your firm's name. Steelline executes the work as part of your team, for agencies and prime contractors extending their bench.

FAQ

Frequently asked.

Why work with a vendor partner instead of hiring direct?

You contract with Steelline Security Consulting LLC rather than hiring an individual. You get senior Azure and identity capability for a defined scope and timeline, without adding headcount, benefits, or long-term commitment. See the partnership types above.

How do you scope an engagement?

Every engagement starts with a discovery call, followed by a written SOW covering deliverables, rate, and timeline before any billable work begins. See the process above.

How is pricing set?

Pricing depends on scope and partnership type, and is confirmed in the SOW before work starts.

Where are you based, and do you work on-site?

Based in McDonough, GA (Atlanta metro), serving clients nationwide. Engagements are remote-first, with on-site availability in the Atlanta metro area as needed.

Do you work with staffing agencies and prime contractors?

Yes. Staffing partners, prime contractors, and direct client engagements are all in scope.
