FAQ

Frequently asked.

What does "C2C" mean, and why work with an LLC instead of hiring direct?

Corp-to-corp: you contract with Steelline Security Consulting LLC rather than hiring an individual. You get senior IAM/Azure capability for a defined scope and timeline, without adding headcount, benefits, or long-term commitment.

How do you scope an engagement?

Every engagement starts with a discovery call, followed by a written SOW covering deliverables, rate, and timeline before any billable work begins — see the process above.

What's your rate?

$100–$120/hr depending on scope: IAM and CyberArk/PAM work leads at $115/hr, general Azure administration at $100/hr. Rate is confirmed in the SOW before work starts.

Where are you based, and do you work on-site?

Based in McDonough, GA (Atlanta metro). Engagements are remote-first, with on-site availability in the Atlanta metro area as needed.

Do you work with staffing agencies?

Yes — both staffing-agency placements and direct client engagements are in scope.
